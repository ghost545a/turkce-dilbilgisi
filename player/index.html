    Automatic subtitles BETA

    Turns the Turkish speech in the open video into timed Turkish subtitles, then translates every line into English.

    Word-error figures are from our test on 24 Turkish news-style clips (Google FLEURS); real videos vary. Models download once from Hugging Face and are normally cached by the browser. Nothing you play leaves this device.

    The key is sent only to the provider you pick, straight from your browser. The audio of the video is uploaded to that provider.
